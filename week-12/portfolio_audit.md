## Purpose

This document presents a structured audit of my Phase 1 cybersecurity portfolio. The audit verifies folder structure, confirms artifact placement, and provides weekly reflections on the skills developed throughout the phase.

## Repository Structure Verification

The repository follows the required structure, with weekly folders labeled week-01 through week-12. Each folder contains the session artifacts produced during that week and a notes.md file documenting the work. The repository visibility is set to Public.

## Audit Findings

The majority of weekly artifacts are present and correctly placed within their respective folders. The strongest sections are the offensive security weeks (Weeks 7 through 9) and the active defense week (Week 11), which contain complete artifact sets demonstrating both technical execution and written analysis. Week 5 and Week 10 artifacts are being consolidated into their folders.

## Weekly Reflection Summary

Across Phase 1, I progressed from foundational Linux administration to full-spectrum offensive and defensive security operations. Early weeks established the fundamentals of the command line, networking, and scripting. Middle weeks introduced containerization, identity management, and a midterm capstone. Later weeks focused on reconnaissance, exploitation, web application attacks, digital forensics, and active defense. The Final Reckoning required applying the complete skillset in a single solo operation.

## Conclusion

This portfolio represents the body of technical work completed during Phase 1 of the Innovation Fellowship. It demonstrates competency across system administration, networking, scripting, containerization, offensive security, digital forensics, and active defense. The repository is organized, version-controlled, and publicly accessible.
